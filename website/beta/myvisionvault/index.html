The app

A private vault
for your photos and videos.

Copy the pictures you want to keep private into a vault that only opens with your PIN or pattern, on your own phone. Nothing is sent to us or shared, and the optional backup stays encrypted in a folder you choose.

Encrypted on this device

Every photo and video is encrypted on your iPhone or iPad with AES-256-GCM, each with its own key.

No account, no servers

No sign-up and no email. There are no servers to send anything to, and the vault works in airplane mode.

Find photos by the words in them

Search for a shop name, a price or a Wi-Fi password, or for what a photo shows. Text and objects are recognized on the phone itself, and the results stay sealed in the vault.

A backup only you can open

Optional and free. Already-encrypted copies go to a folder you choose, such as iCloud Drive, and only your 52-character recovery key opens them. Restore after deleting the app or on a new phone.

A camera inside the vault

Shoot photos and videos straight into the vault. They never land in your photo library, and location data is removed.

Move to a new phone, free

Send your vault directly from your old phone to your new one over your own Wi-Fi, encrypted the whole way. Works between iPhone and Android once the Android version is out.

Principles

The rules we
built it on.

These don’t change from beta to release.

  1. Your photos stay yours.

    They are encrypted and kept on your phone. We never receive a copy. If you turn on backup, only already-encrypted copies go to a folder you choose.

  2. You hold the only key.

    Nobody, including us, can reset your PIN or open your vault. There is no back door.

  3. We collect nothing.

    No account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking. PINs, file names and keys are never written to logs.

  4. Things go in, nothing comes out.

    There is no share, export, save or print. Files leave the vault only still encrypted: to your own new phone, or to your own backup folder.

  5. The app never deletes your photos on its own.

    Importing only copies, so your originals stay in your gallery. Wrong PINs make you wait, but never erase the vault. Moving to a new phone never deletes anything on the old one.

  6. Honest about limits.

    We say what the app protects and what it can’t. No “unhackable” claims.

Before you test

Keep your own copies

This is a beta and may have bugs. Only put in photos you also have saved somewhere else.

Remember your PIN, keep your recovery key

Nobody can reset a forgotten PIN or pattern, including us. With the encrypted backup and your recovery key you can start over and restore. Without them, the photos can’t be recovered.

Wrong PINs make you wait

After three wrong tries, each new try waits 1 minute, then 5, then 15, then an hour. Your files stay safe while you wait.

Originals stay in your gallery

Importing copies. If you want an original gone, delete it in Photos, then from Recently Deleted and any cloud photo backup.
