A PIN alone
won’t open it.

Your PIN is turned into a key with Argon2id, then combined with a key held inside the phone’s security chip. The vault opens only when both are there. Your remaining attempts are always on screen.

Argon2idSecure EnclaveStrongBox

03 / 06

Move photos
into the vault.

Each photo and video you pick is encrypted with its own key, then decrypted again to check it. If you turned on “Delete originals after import”, only then does the app ask your photo library to delete the original. If anything fails, the original stays where it was.

AES-256-GCMPer-file keysDelete after verify

04 / 06

In the vault

Photos and videos shot with the vault camera never touch your photo library. Video is encrypted in chunks while it records, and location data is removed.

Never saved to your photo libraryLocation removed

05 / 06

Viewed only
inside the app.

There is no share, export, save or print button. The vault locks the moment you leave the app, and its contents are hidden in the app switcher.

and the key is gone.

After three wrong PINs in a row, the key that opens the vault is erased from the phone. From then on, neither the right PIN nor a reinstall will open the files.

Security

A door that needs
two keys.

The master key can be unlocked only when your PIN and the phone’s security chip are both present. Neither one opens the vault alone. The unlocked master key is kept in memory only while the vault is open, and erased when it locks.

Three tries, then the key is destroyed

attempt counter written first

Each attempt is recorded before the PIN is checked, so force-quitting the app mid-check doesn’t reset the count.

No internet, by design

no network permission

The Android app has no internet permission at all. There are no servers, accounts, cloud, ads or analytics, and every feature works in airplane mode.

A one-way vault

one-way import

Share, export and open-in-another-app don’t exist in the code. Files that enter the vault can only be viewed inside the app.

A camera inside the vault

in-vault camera

Photos skip the photo library entirely. Video is encrypted chunk by chunk as it records, so no unencrypted file is left in storage.

Reinstalling won’t open it

device-only keys

Keys are stored with a device-only protection setting, so they’re left out of backups and device transfers. Delete and reinstall the app and you start with a new, empty vault.

Video, verified chunk by chunk

chunked AES-GCM streaming

Video is decrypted a little at a time, in memory, as it plays. Each chunk carries an authentication tag, so reordered or truncated video stops playing.

Good to know

A strong vault
still has limits.

No app can promise it can never be broken, and PrivateVault doesn’t. Here is what it protects, and what it can’t.

There is no recovery

Forget your PIN, lose your phone or delete the app, and the files can’t be opened again. We can’t open them for you either.

Jailbroken or rooted phones

The three-try limit holds on an unmodified phone. An attacker with a jailbroken or rooted phone, or professional forensic tools, can get around it. The app blocks access to the vault when it detects such a phone, and we recommend a PIN of 10 or more digits.

While the vault is open

If someone takes control of the phone while the vault is open, no app can stop them. Lock the vault when you’re done.

Copies left elsewhere

You need to delete copies left in Recently Deleted (or Trash) and in cloud photo backups yourself. The app reminds you after each import.
