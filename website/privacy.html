Privacy Policy

Effective date: [launch date] · Operated by JellyDock

Summary

The PrivateVault app does not collect personal data and does not send it anywhere. There are no servers and no accounts. Photos and videos you put in the vault are encrypted and stored only on your phone. The app is designed so that no one, including us at JellyDock, can open them without your PIN (or pattern) and your phone.

1. Information the app handles

The app processes the following on your phone only. None of it is sent to us.

  • Photos and videos in the vault, with their file names, capture details and thumbnails. Each file is encrypted with its own key (AES-256-GCM) and kept in the app’s storage. Capture details of files imported from your photo library, including location, are kept as they are, encrypted.
  • Your PIN or pattern. It is never stored as entered. It is used only to derive the key that opens the vault (Argon2id), and the vault opens only together with a key held in your phone’s security chip.
  • Failed unlock count and app settings, needed for the three-attempt key destruction and for the app to work.

3. Collection, use and sharing

Because the app collects no personal data, we have none to use or share with anyone. The app contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting tools, and no outside company processes data on our behalf.

We may receive only the following:

  • Support email. If you email us, we receive your email address and message. We use them only to reply, and delete them once your request is resolved, unless a law requires us to keep them longer.
  • Information from the app stores. Under their own policies, Apple’s App Store and Google Play may give developers crash reports and statistics from users who agreed to share them. Apple and Google process this information and provide it in a form that does not identify you. If the app is paid, payments are handled by the store.

4. Backups and device transfers

Vault data and keys are excluded from iCloud and iTunes backups, Google backups and device-to-device transfers. If you change or lose your phone, the vault can’t be moved or restored.

Originals you move into the vault may still exist in your photo library’s Recently Deleted (trash), or as copies already uploaded to iCloud Photos or Google Photos. The app can’t delete those copies. You need to delete them yourself.

5. Retention and deletion

Files in the vault stay on your phone until you delete them. Data or keys are deleted in these cases, and there is no way to recover them:

  • When you permanently delete a file: its key, the encrypted file and its thumbnail are deleted.
  • When you reset the vault, or enter a wrong PIN or pattern three times in a row: the key that opens the vault is deleted.
  • When you delete the app: the app’s storage is erased. If you reinstall, any leftover keys are wiped and you start with a new vault.

7. Security measures

All app data is encrypted with a separate key per file. The keys can be unlocked only when your PIN and your phone’s security chip (Secure Enclave or StrongBox) are both present. The app makes no network connections (the Android app has no internet permission at all), so there is no path for data to leave the device. No independent security audit has been done yet.

8. Your rights

Everything the app stores is on your phone, so you can view and delete it directly in the app. We hold none of your data, so we can’t view or recover it for you.

9. Children

The app collects no personal data from anyone, of any age.
